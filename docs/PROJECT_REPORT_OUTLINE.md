# Suggested academic report outline

1. **Abstract** — problem, dataset, methods and key project deliverables.
2. **Introduction** — healthcare encounter analytics and the MDM concepts demonstrated.
3. **Dataset description** — source, encounter grain, fields, labels and limitations.
4. **Data cleaning and profiling** — placeholder handling, duplicates, missingness, excluded columns and validation outputs.
5. **Data warehouse design** — business process, fact grain, star schema, dimensions, measures, keys and ETL.
6. **OLAP implementation** — explain roll-up, drill-down, slice, dice and pivot with screenshots/CSV examples.
7. **Exploratory analysis** — distributions, readmission mix and clinical/medication summaries.
8. **Decision-tree algorithms** — ID3, C4.5-style gain ratio and Gini-based tree; split strategy and evaluation.
9. **Data mining** — transaction construction, vertical TID representation, Apriori, FP-Growth and association metrics.
10. **Regression** — target, features, train/test method and MAE/RMSE/R².
11. **Dashboard** — pages, filters, charts and OLAP explorer.
12. **Results and discussion** — findings with caution against causal/clinical interpretation.
13. **Limitations and future work** — no encounter date, high-cardinality dimensions, educational tree variants, data age and potential extension to a date-bearing source.
14. **Conclusion and references**.
